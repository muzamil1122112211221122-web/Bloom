01 / 04 The Genesis

THE BLOOM

A single rose, grown for one purpose.

Harvested at dawn in the historic valleys of Grasse when dew-laden petals hold their deepest velvet luster. Each blossom is selected by hand, yielding a chromatic purity impossible to fabricate through synthetic chemistry.

02 / 04 Extraction

THE RELEASE

Petals fall and dissolve into liquid pigment.

Under gentle cellular cold-pressing, delicate botanical membranes yield their sacred crimson essence. The living pigment is suspended in bio-compatible squalane, preserving the raw vitality and luminous warmth of petals in mid-flight.

03 / 04 Architecture

THE FORM

Pigment sets into a precise matte bullet.

Poured into bespoke cryogenic molds with microscopic architectural precision. As the formulation crystallizes, it captures an ultra-fine matte silhouette engineered to glide with zero resistance and embrace the lip's natural contour.

04 / 04 Application

THE FINISH

One continuous motion from pickup to application.

A sensorial transformation upon first touch. The bullet yields a weightless veil of multidimensional color that breathes with your lips throughout the day—unfading, unyielding, unmistakably couture.
